About Us

Every pastor’s second most important network

Because we focus on every pastor’s most important priority — prayer and the ministry of the Word.

The 6:4 Fellowship exists to catalyze pastors, around the world, to Prayer-and-Word-Powered Ministry through an urgent call to the hope of a spiritual awakening, and by helping them experience a life-changing rhythm of deeper conviction, rich community, and growing capacity for Spirit-empowered ministry.

Our vision is to see pastors and churches served with the encouragement and equipment necessary to catalyze Acts 6:3 disciples through Acts 6:4 leaders in the hope of an Acts 6:7 awakening. We call it a divine pattern:

6:4 + 6:3 = 6:7

Who we are

A pastor-to-pastor community

The 6:4 Fellowship is a pastor-led, biblically-based, international and multi-denominational network of pastors rooted in the biblical paradigm of ministry seen in Acts 6:1–7.

The name “6:4 Fellowship” is taken from the two vital priorities found in Acts 6:4: prayer and the ministry of the Word. These priorities defined New Testament leadership and gave early church leaders the courage to say “no” to the distractions of lesser demands in order to focus on the core priorities of supernatural ministry.

It is not oriented around a single ministry or personality. It is cross-denominational and led by a team of pastors who exemplify the values of Acts 6:4. We are not focused as much on what pastors are doing but on what they are becoming. When we unite our hearts around a vision for a church committed to prayer and the ministry of the Word, through the power of God’s Spirit, things will change. Christ is honored and his mission for this world is accomplished.

What we do

Health and hope through prayer-and-Word-powered paths

The 6:4 Fellowship seeks to help pastors experience more health and hope through four connected paths.

Conviction

Pastors are often introduced through connection with others in the same community or network. That connection is enriched by “The Power of Yes” — an interactive, two-session leadership conversation reintroducing Prayer-and-Word-Powered ministry. These sessions provide introductory principles and a “test-drive” with the 6:4 team to embrace lasting conviction about Acts 6:4 leadership aiming for an Acts 6:7 outcome — and regular Scripture-fed, Spirit-led, worship-based prayer.

Community

Our strategy facilitates rich, authentic engagement with a diverse community of like-minded pastors committed to the same biblical principles — through local, regional, and national experiences of prayer, equipping, and fellowship that strengthen one another personally and corporately.

Capacity

Members gain access to biblical resources — videos, prayer guides, and instructional articles — plus special training experiences locally, regionally, nationally, and virtually that cultivate dependence on God and competency in leading prayer-and-Word-powered transformation.

Coaching

The central feature is The Praying Leader — a coaching and mentoring experience for pastors. Additional coaching cultivates personal, marriage, and ministry health throughout the fellowship.
