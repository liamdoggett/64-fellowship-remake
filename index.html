Acts 6:1–7

Fellowship and pray with like-minded pastors

We are a community of pastors around the world, doing ministry in the hope of a spiritual awakening — sharpening one another toward deeper conviction, rich community, and growing capacity for Spirit-empowered ministry.

Our mission is rooted in the biblical paradigm of ministry in Acts 6. We seek to empower 6:4 leaders, encourage 6:3 disciples, and envision a 6:7 awakening.

6:4 + 6:3 = 6:7

We call it a divine pattern.

Scripture-fed, Spirit-led, worship-based prayer

Prayer-and-Word-Powered

Health and hope through three paths

The 6:4 Fellowship helps pastors experience more health and hope through prayer-and-Word-powered paths.

Conviction

We model and train Scripture-fed, Spirit-led, worship-based prayer — so pastors embrace lasting conviction about the priority of Acts 6:4 leadership aiming for an Acts 6:7 outcome.

Community

Rich, authentic engagement with a diverse, multi-denominational community of pastors committed to the same vital biblical principles.

Capacity

We build growing capacity in every pastor’s prayer-and-Word-powered leadership through coaching, resources, and gatherings.
